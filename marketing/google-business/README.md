# Google Business image set

Finished, upload-ready images for the Cloud Secure Canada Google Business
Profile. The photography is licensed stock already used by the website; no
people, offices, equipment, or client engagements were generated or presented
as real Cloud Secure Canada operations.

The wide cover keeps the logo and primary message within a conservative safe
area, but Google can crop cover photos differently by device. Preview the crop
inside Business Profile before publishing.

## Google image requirements

All finished files are JPG or PNG, at least 720 pixels on each required edge,
and below Google's 5 MB maximum. Google's current photo guidance is available
at <https://support.google.com/business/answer/6103862>.

## Suggested post captions

### Incident response

When an incident becomes operational, clarity matters. Cloud Secure Canada
helps teams contain the threat, investigate what happened, and move into a
controlled recovery. Available from Toronto for remote engagements across
Canada, the US, and the UK.

### Penetration testing

Find the gaps before attackers do. Our authorized penetration tests focus on
credible attack paths, clear evidence, and remediation guidance your technical
team can act on.

### SOC and detection engineering

More alerts do not automatically create better security. We help teams improve
coverage, tune noisy detections, and build response workflows that analysts can
trust under pressure.

### Device hardening

Reduce avoidable exposure with practical endpoint and device baselines. We
review configuration, access, logging, and operational controls around the
systems your team depends on.

### Infrastructure security

Cloud, network, and platform reviews built around real risk. Cloud Secure
Canada helps organizations identify weak controls, prioritize improvements,
and strengthen the systems behind the business.
